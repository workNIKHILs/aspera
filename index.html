ABOUT OUR MOVEMENT

MORE THAN A CLUB. A CULTURE OF INNOVATION.

ASPERA is the Entrepreneurship Cell of Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering, Mysuru. It acts as a student-driven community backed by the institution to foster a vibrant startup and business culture.

  • Startup Culture & Innovation Cultivating creative ideation and converting engineering problem statements into real-world business models.
  • Mentorship & Industry Linkage Direct guidance from seasoned entrepreneurs, startup founders, institutional leaders, and corporate executives.
  • Student Leadership & Team Building Hands-on execution experience organizing campus initiatives, venture challenges, and collaborative cohorts.
